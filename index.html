Everything you check in the web interface, ready in a tap.

Built for people who run a NAS at home or in a lab and want to know it's healthy without sitting at a computer.

Dashboard

CPU, memory, storage, CPU temperature, uptime, load and network throughput, with a health score and active alerts. Figures match what openmediavault itself reports.

Storage and disk health

Every mounted volume and disk, with openmediavault's S.M.A.R.T. verdict for each drive, temperatures, power-on hours and the full attribute table.

Docker and Compose

Containers with status, CPU and memory, live logs, start, stop and restart, Compose stacks and guided deployment. Needs the openmediavault Compose plugin.

Logs

System, authentication and service logs in plain language, with search and level filters, so the line that matters is easy to find.

History and forecasts

Up to 30 days of trends, stored only on your device, and an estimate of when storage will fill once there's enough data to be sure.

Widgets and Wake NAS

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets for one server or all of them, and a Wake NAS button and Siri shortcut that start a sleeping server over your network.

At home on iPhone and iPad.

On iPad the dashboard spreads across two columns with a sidebar, and works in Split View, Slide Over and Stage Manager. Keyboard shortcuts cover refresh, servers, settings and tabs.

Questions

What do I need?

An iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later, and an openmediavault 7 or 8 server you can reach from it — on the same network, or over a VPN. Docker features need the openmediavault Compose plugin; the CPU temperature reading needs the CPU Temperature plugin.

Can I try it without a server?

Yes. Tap Try Demo NAS on the dashboard to explore every screen with sample data. Nothing is sent over the network.

Which account should I sign in with?

An openmediavault administrator account shows everything. A read-only account can connect, but openmediavault only gives CPU, memory, disk health and network figures to administrators, and changes always need an administrator.

Does it work away from home?

It works anywhere your device can reach the server, such as through a VPN like WireGuard or Tailscale. Exposing the openmediavault web interface directly to the internet isn't recommended.

Is this an official openmediavault app?

No. OMV Remote is an independent app, made by a user for users. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by the openmediavault project.
